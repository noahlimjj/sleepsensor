Privacy Policy for SleepSensor

Last updated: October 13, 2026

At SleepSensor, your privacy is our core design principle. SleepSensor is designed to monitor snoring and teeth grinding (bruxism) using real-time, on-device machine learning. We believe that your intimate sleep audio should never leave your personal device.

1. Microphone Audio & On-Device Processing

SleepSensor requests access to your microphone solely to analyze sound patterns (such as snoring and bruxism) while you sleep.

  • 100% On-Device Analysis: All audio feature extraction and machine learning classification run entirely on your phone via on-device models.
  • Zero Audio Uploads: Raw microphone audio is never recorded to remote servers, uploaded to the cloud, or shared with third parties.
  • Temporary Audio Clips: Any audio highlights saved for user replay are stored exclusively in your device's local storage and can be deleted at any time.

2. Health & Sleep Data Storage

Your sleep metrics (event timestamps, snore count, grinding count, sleep duration) are stored locally in your browser/device database (IndexedDB). If you choose to enable cloud backup via account sign-in, only summarized numerical stats and metadata are synced — never raw audio recordings.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer your personal data or audio to advertisers, data brokers, or any third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can delete your sleep sessions, recorded audio highlights, or reset the app database at any time directly within the app settings.

5. Medical Disclaimer

SleepSensor is intended for wellness, lifestyle, and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition (such as obstructive sleep apnea). Consult a qualified physician for any health concerns.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or SleepSensor's privacy practices, please contact us via our GitHub repository: https://github.com/noahlimjj/sleepsensor.
